## Enquiry behaviour

The forms do not deliver leads. Preparing a summary validates required fields and displays a local read-only summary. Copying requires a separate explicit click and copies only the current form values. Clipboard failure falls back to manual selection. No form values are placed in URLs, localStorage, analytics or network requests. Telephone links express intent; they do not prove a connected call.

No GA4/GTM ID is installed. Do not report `generate_lead` until an actual endpoint confirms receipt. Later tracking must distinguish service views, contact clicks, local summaries, received enquiries and qualified leads, without sending personal details into analytics.
