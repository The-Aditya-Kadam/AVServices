FAQ

Digital marketing questions

What is AEO (answer engine optimisation)?

AEO is the work of making your business the answer that AI assistants and search features give. It uses clear question-and-answer content, structured data and trusted mentions so tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can understand and cite you.

How is AI-based SEO different from normal SEO?

Normal SEO focuses on ranking in the ten blue links. AI-based SEO also prepares your content to be picked up and quoted in AI-generated answers, and uses AI tools to research topics and spot gaps faster.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most sites see early movement in two to three months and stronger results in six months or more. It depends on your competition, your site's starting point and how much content we publish.

Can you make my website load fast?

Yes. We build and optimise sites to score 90+ on Google PageSpeed for mobile, which helps both rankings and conversions.

Do you run ads and social media for products?

Yes. We plan and run product campaigns on Google and social media, with tracking so you can see what each rupee brings back.
