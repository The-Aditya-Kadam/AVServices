FAQ

Insurance questions, answered

Is AVServices an insurance company?

No. AVServices helps you choose and buy the right insurance plan. The policy is issued by the insurer, and all benefits and terms are as per the policy document.

How much life cover do I need?

A common rule of thumb is 10 to 15 times your yearly income, plus any loans. We look at your income, debts and goals to suggest a sensible amount.

What is the difference between a term plan and a ULIP?

A term plan gives pure life cover at a low premium and pays out only on death during the term. A ULIP combines life cover with market-linked investment, so returns can go up or down.

Do you help with claims?

Yes. We help you with the claim forms and documents and follow up with the insurer until the claim is settled.

Can I get tax benefits on these plans?

Many life and health insurance premiums are eligible for tax benefits under the Income Tax Act, subject to current rules and limits. We explain what applies to your plan.
