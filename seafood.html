FAQ

Seafood trade questions

What types of seafood do you trade?

We handle all types of fish and seafood, including fresh and frozen fish, dried fish, shrimp and prawns, squid and cuttlefish, and crab. Tell us the species, size and grade you need.

Do you handle both import and export?

Yes. We source seafood for export to overseas buyers and also import seafood for buyers in India, depending on the order.

What is your minimum order quantity?

It depends on the product and destination. Share your requirement and we will confirm the minimum quantity, packing and price.

How do you keep seafood fresh during shipping?

Frozen products move in a temperature-controlled cold chain, from processing and storage to reefer containers. Fresh and chilled products are packed with ice or gel packs for fast transport.

Which documents do you provide with shipments?

We arrange the documents your shipment needs, such as invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, health certificate and bill of lading, as required by the destination country.
